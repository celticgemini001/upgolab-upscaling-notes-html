Denoise before upscale: the ordering mistake that costs detail

Most blurry footage is not short of pixels. It is short of signal. Upscaling interpolates, so it enlarges compression blocks and sensor grain along with everything you wanted to recover.

The order that works

  1. Stabilise the clip.
  2. Denoise with a temporal filter, not a spatial one.
  3. Upscale at 2x, not 4x. Two times is usually the information ceiling.
  4. Sharpen last, gently.

Why 4x first is worse

A 4x model applied to noisy footage enlarges the noise. You then spend more time cleaning an upscaled mess than you would have spent denoising the original.
